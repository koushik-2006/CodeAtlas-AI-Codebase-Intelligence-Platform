## 2. Institutional Formatting Verification

- [ ] **College Logo**: Anna University guidelines specify that the official college emblem may be placed on the cover page if approved by the department. If required, insert the V.S.B. Engineering College crest above the department name.
- [ ] **Font & Sizing**:
  - Title: 20–22 pt, Bold, Centered, Times New Roman.
  - Chapter Headings: 16–18 pt, Bold, All Caps, Centered, Times New Roman.
  - Section Headings: 14 pt, Bold, Left-Aligned, Times New Roman.
  - Subsection Headings: 12 pt, Bold, Left-Aligned, Times New Roman.
  - Body Text: 11–12 pt, Justified, 1.5 Line Spacing, Times New Roman.
  - Margins: Left 1.25 in (for binding), Right 1.0 in, Top 1.0 in, Bottom 1.0 in.
- [ ] **Page Numbering**:
  - Preliminary Pages: Lower-case Roman numerals (`i, ii, iii, iv, v, vi, vii, viii, ix, x`).
  - Chapter Pages: Arabic numerals beginning with `1` on Chapter 1.
- [ ] **Signatures**:
  - [ ] Project Supervisor signature on Bonafide Certificate.
  - [ ] Head of the Department signature on Bonafide Certificate.
  - [ ] Student signatures on Declaration.
  - [ ] Internal and External Examiners' signatures during the Viva-Voce examination.
